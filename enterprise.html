The problem

Most financial-wellness benefits go unused.

Financially stressed employees are distracted, less secure, and harder to retain. Yet the tools meant to help — long courses, dense dashboards, generic webinars — feel like homework, so people never open them. The result: a benefit you pay for that quietly sits idle.

NorthReserve takes the opposite approach. It shows an employee their whole financial picture in one calm, visual moment, and points to a single next step. It's the difference between a benefit that's offered and one that's used.

Why teams choose it

Built to be opened, not endured.

01

Private by design

Employees enter their own numbers on their own device. You never see an individual's finances — only anonymous, aggregate engagement. That's what makes people willing to try it.

02

Clear for every literacy level

No jargon, no spreadsheets. A landscape anyone can read at a glance — whether they've budgeted for years or never once.

03

Calm, not alarming

Facts first, then one achievable step. It reduces financial anxiety instead of amplifying it — the tone your people will thank you for.

04

Fast to roll out

Nothing to install on your systems. Share a link, and your team is standing on their mountains the same week.

How it works for teams

Three steps, start to insight.

01

You share access

We give your team a private link or code. No employee data, credentials, or systems access required from you.

02

Employees see their mountain

Each person enters a few numbers privately and gets their whole picture — plus the one step that matters most for them.

03

You see engagement

An anonymized snapshot of how many engaged and where your team feels the most pressure — never individual figures.
