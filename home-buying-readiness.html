Questions people ask

What counts as "ready"?

Steady income, manageable debt, a down payment, cash reserves left after closing, and decent credit. This tool weighs all five so you can see which one is holding you back — before a lender does.

What DTI do lenders look for?

Often a total debt-to-income ratio around 43% or lower, most comfortable near 36% — your monthly debts plus the new housing payment, divided by gross monthly income.
