Questions people ask

How many months do I actually need?

Three to six months of essential expenses is the common range — not a percentage of income. One month is a huge first win; build in milestones from there.

Does my checking buffer count?

Keep them separate. A ~$500–$1,000 buffer in checking handles everyday timing; the emergency fund is larger and for a real loss of income.
